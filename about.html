Our story

Why we do design and build under one roof

Most remodeling frustration comes from handoffs: the plumber is waiting on the electrician, who is waiting on the tile setter. We built Rain City to remove the handoffs.

Our team plans the project, orders the materials and performs the plumbing, electrical, heating and finish work. When the same company owns every step, schedules hold and quality stays consistent.
