About Rain City

A builder who answers the phone and finishes the job

Rain City Design & Build is a licensed construction company for homeowners who want remodeling done properly, without the juggling act of hiring five different trades.

We design the plan, pull the permits and do the plumbing, electrical, heating and finish work ourselves. That means fewer delays, fewer surprises and one person to call from the first walkthrough to the final inspection.

Good to know

Questions before you book

All questions
Are you licensed, bonded and insured?

Yes. Rain City Design & Build, LLC is licensed, bonded and insured for the plumbing, electrical and remodeling work we perform. Proof of insurance is available on request before work begins.

How does the online quote work?

Choose your services, enter approximate sizes and finish level, and the calculator shows a live estimate range. Book a preferred date and the full quote is emailed to you while our office receives a copy. Final pricing is confirmed after a walkthrough.

Do you handle permits?

We pull the permits that your project requires and schedule inspections, so you are not left navigating city paperwork.

Can one team really do plumbing, electrical and HVAC?

That is exactly why clients hire us. Trades are coordinated under one schedule, so a bathroom remodel does not stall waiting for an electrician or a plumber to become available.

How long will my project take?

A sump pump or water heater swap is usually a single visit. A bathroom remodel typically runs a couple of weeks once materials are on site; larger remodels are scheduled in phases. Your written plan includes dates.

Ready to see your project priced?

Build your estimate in two minutes, then pick a walkthrough date. Your quote lands in your inbox right away.
