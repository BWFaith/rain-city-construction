Our process

A calm, predictable path from first call to final walk-through.

Five steps

What happens, and when

Walkthrough

We visit, measure and listen. You leave with clear next steps, not a sales pitch.

Quote & plan

A written scope, itemized price and schedule. Materials and finishes are chosen together.

Permits & prep

We handle permits, order materials and protect your home before the first tool comes out.

Build

Demolition through finishes by one coordinated crew, with daily clean-up and progress updates.

Final walk & care

We walk the finished work with you, fix any punch-list items and leave you with care instructions.

On site

How we protect your home

  • Floors covered with protective paper and film
  • Windows, doors and fixtures masked
  • Dust containment where demolition occurs
  • Daily clean-up and tools stored at day end
  • Final detail clean before hand-over
Before we start

Your preparation checklist

Clear the work area

Move valuables and small items out of the room. We move and protect large furniture.

Confirm access

Tell us about parking, building rules and the best entrance.

Review the scope

Read your written quote and mark any questions before the start date.

Choose a decision-maker

One person who can approve selections and change orders keeps things moving.

After the work

Warranty and aftercare

You receive care instructions, product and equipment information, and a direct line back to us if anything needs attention. Add your exact warranty terms here.
